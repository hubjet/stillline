Privacy Policy

Stillline is a personal timeline for the moments you want to remember. Here is how the app handles the entries, dates, categories, and photos you choose to add.

1. Information You Create in the App

Stillline allows you to record personal moments by creating entries, adding dates and times, organizing entries into categories, and attaching photos.

Depending on how you use the App, information stored by the App may include:

  • Entry content: titles, descriptions, dates, and times you enter.
  • Categories and record-management information: categories you create or select, as well as information associated with active, deleted, or trashed entries.
  • Photos: images you select from your photo library or capture with your camera and attach to entries.
  • App-related data: files and other information needed to store, display, and manage your entries and photos.

The App does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

2. How and Where Your Information Is Stored

Stillline is designed to store your timeline content locally on your device. Based on the current implementation:

  • Entries, categories, and trash data are stored as JSON files in the App’s local Application Support directory.
  • Photos attached to entries are stored as JPEG files in the App’s local Documents directory.
  • Temporary in-memory caches may be used to display photos while the App is running.

The current version does not provide its own user account system, server-side database for timeline content, or cloud synchronization service. Based on our review of the current implementation, we have not identified functionality that uploads your timeline entries or attached photos to a server operated by us.

This description concerns the App’s own data-handling functionality. It does not cover data handling by iOS, device backups, or other services you independently choose to use.

3. Camera and Photo Library

Selecting photos from your library

When you select a photo using the iOS system photo picker, the App processes the selected image and saves a copy in its local storage for display in the relevant timeline entry.

Taking photos with the camera

When you take a photo using the App’s camera feature, the App uses the iOS camera interface. After capture, the App attempts to save the original image to your device’s system photo library and separately saves a processed copy in the App’s local storage for use in your timeline entry.

These are separate operations. Removing a photo from a Stillline entry does not automatically delete the original image saved to your system photo library. You can manage that image separately in the Apple Photos app.

Camera and photo access are subject to the permissions and settings provided by iOS. You can review or change the relevant permissions in your device’s Settings.

4. iCloud and Device Backups

The App does not implement its own iCloud synchronization or cloud storage service.

However, files stored in the App’s local directories may be included in device backups, depending on iOS behavior and your device settings, including whether iCloud Backup is enabled.

Device backups are managed by Apple and are subject to Apple’s services, policies, and your device settings. We do not operate a separate backup service for your timeline content.

You can review and manage your device backups through the relevant settings on your device. Photos saved separately to your system photo library must be managed through the Photos app and the settings for the services you use.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

The current version of Stillline does not include advertising features or intentionally integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

We do not sell your timeline entries or attached photos, and we do not share that content with third parties for their own independent purposes through an App-operated service.

The App uses Apple-provided iOS frameworks for functions such as camera access and photo selection. Apple’s handling of information through its operating system and services is governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

Other services you choose to use, including device backup services, may handle data according to their own policies and settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your entries and attached photos remain in the App’s local storage until you delete them or the App and its associated local data are removed from your device, subject to normal iOS storage and backup behavior.

Deleting individual items

The App provides controls to edit entries, remove photos from entries, move entries to the trash, restore supported entries, and permanently delete items.

The App manages locally stored photo files according to whether they are still referenced by entries or other records. Removing a photo from one entry does not necessarily remove a local file that is still referenced elsewhere.

Removing the App

Deleting Stillline removes the App and its associated local data from the device, subject to iOS behavior. This does not automatically delete photos separately saved to your system photo library or copies that may be retained in device backups.

System-level copies and backups

Photos saved to your system photo library and copies included in device backups must be managed separately through the Photos app and your device or relevant service settings.

The App cannot guarantee that every copy of information will be immediately removed from every backup or other system-managed location when you delete an entry or uninstall the App. For questions about the App’s deletion features, contact us using the email address below.

7. Children’s Privacy

Stillline is a general-purpose personal recording application and is not specifically directed to children.

The App does not require an account, and the current implementation does not provide a service for transmitting timeline entries or attached photos to a server operated by us.

If the App’s features or intended audience change in a way that affects how children’s information is handled, we will review and update this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

Parents or guardians who have questions about the App’s privacy practices may contact us using the details below.
