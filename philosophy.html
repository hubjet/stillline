Every moment leaves a trace.

Stillline Moments began with a simple idea: every moment deserves to be kept.

A photo taken without thinking. A few words. A feeling, right then.

They don’t need a title. They don’t need a reason. One day, you’ll look back — and they’ll be the thread that connects then to now.

So we made recording simple again.

Simple. And nothing extra.

Native iOS design, the way your iPhone already works. Fewer steps, less decoration.

No feature pile-up. No new habits to learn just to use the app.

Recording should feel natural — not like a task.

Your moments stay yours.

Local first, from day one.

No account. No server. Your entries and photos stay on your device.

Privacy isn’t a feature we added. It’s how the app was built.

Time leaves its trace.

Stillline is more than a way to record. It’s a way to look back.

A timeline, quietly unfolding — life, inspiration, and work, one thread.

Days that felt ordinary then become the ones you treasure most.

Record life. Keep the memories. See the path time has traced.
